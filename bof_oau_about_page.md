# About BOF OAU

## The Students' Professional Bureau of Finance
**Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife, Nigeria**

---

> *"Empowering future financial leaders — one analyst at a time."*

---

Nigeria's **first student-led finance organisation**, founded in 2011. BOF OAU unites passionate students from all faculties at OAU to cultivate the next generation of financial professionals through practical training, competitions, and real-world exposure.

## Our Vision

To be Africa's leading student-led finance organisation, producing world-class financial professionals with technical expertise, innovative thinking, and ethical leadership.

## Our Mission

To empower students with practical financial skills, industry exposure, and leadership development through cutting-edge training, competitions, and networking opportunities.

---

## What We Do

### 🏆 CFA Institute Challenges
Consistent top-three finishes in the global CFA Research Challenge — one of the most prestigious student finance competitions in the world.

### 🏦 Corporate Strategy & M&A
Hands-on exposure to investment banking, deal structuring, and corporate finance through real-world case simulations.

### 💻 Fintech & Innovation
Hackathons, workshops, and projects at the intersection of technology and financial services.

### 🤝 Finance 360 Conference
Our flagship annual event bringing together students, professionals, and industry leaders for insight and networking.

### 📅 Biweekly General Meetings
Regular sessions to learn, connect, collaborate, and stay updated on global finance developments.

## FAQs

**What is BOF OAU?**
Nigeria's first student-led finance organisation at OAU, founded in 2011. We focus on practical finance skills, not just theory.

**Who can join?**
Any student at Obafemi Awolowo University with a passion for finance — regardless of faculty or department.

**How do I become a member?**
Fill out the application form on our website. Our team will review your application and follow up with you.

**What training programmes are available?**
Investment strategy workshops, financial modelling sessions, industry-specific seminars, and mentorship programmes with finance professionals.

**What are BOF OAU's achievements?**
Consistent top-three finishes in global competitions including the CFA Research Challenge, and over 20 major competition victories since 2011.
